# Arcane Quantum & Space Lab

**Research and specifications for trusted information.**

Arcane Quantum & Space Lab (Arcane-QSL), part of Arcane Cyber, LLC, advances research in digital trust, post-quantum security, and space applications.

## Trusted Protected Object Specification

Our current specification project is the **Trusted Protected Object (TPO)**: a vendor-neutral logical model for packaging digital payloads with cryptographic integrity evidence and authenticated metadata, with optional confidentiality, policy expressions, credentials, and digital-origin evidence.

We are preparing **v0.7 for community review**. The project is in development; public release, licensing, and contribution arrangements are being finalized. This is not an implementation-stable or accredited standards-body publication.

TPO is intended to support independent implementations. It does not mandate an Arcane Cyber product, cloud provider, or key-management system.
